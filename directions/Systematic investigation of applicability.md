# Systematic investigation of applicability

**Status: candidate; not selected.**

Take one promising transformation and investigate how its validity and benefit change with shapes, types, layouts or compiler settings.

A framework could coordinate related experiments and preserve their conditions and results, producing an empirical map of where an optimization helps.

Open questions: which dimensions matter, how much testing is affordable, and what conclusions would the collected evidence support?

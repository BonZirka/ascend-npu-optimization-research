# Q01 — Taxonomy of bug finding and performance search

**Status: open; research deferred.**

**Question:** Is there an existing taxonomy of testing and analysis methods that we can reuse to describe bug finding, performance regression detection, and searches for missed optimizations?

## Context

[[questions/H02 - How is it done today|H02]] uses the phrase “proactive searches.” We need to investigate whether established terminology can help describe current practices and position the proposed framework. No taxonomy has been selected, and “proactive” is not yet an adopted classification.

## What to investigate

- Find relevant taxonomies or surveys in software testing, compiler testing and performance analysis, with sources for their definitions.
- Examine how they distinguish detecting regressions, investigating existing problems and searching for improvement opportunities.
- Check whether “proactive” and “reactive” are established categories in this context and what they mean.
- Assess which distinctions apply to AscendNPU-IR practices and the proposed framework, and where they do not fit.

## Expected outcome

A short, sourced account of reusable terminology and its limitations, with a proposed mapping to our research. The mapping will remain subject to review.

# Q05 — Proactive optimization discovery in literature and other compiler projects

**Status: open; research deferred.**

**Question:** What approaches to proactive discovery of missed optimizations exist in the literature and other compiler projects, and what could we learn from them?

## Context

[[questions/Q03 - Existing proactive searches for missed optimizations|Q03]] concerns the AscendNPU-IR team's current practices. This question looks beyond that team for methods that could inform the proposed framework.

The terminology and classification of these methods are also subject to investigation in [[questions/Q01 - Taxonomy of bug finding and performance search|Q01 — Taxonomy of bug finding and performance search]].

## What to investigate

- Identify approaches described in research papers and documented compiler projects.
- Examine how they find candidates, check correctness and establish evidence of an improvement.
- Assess their requirements, limitations and possible applicability to AscendNPU-IR.

## Expected outcome

A sourced comparison of relevant methods, identifying possible reuse and unresolved applicability questions. Investigating a method does not commit the project to implementing it.

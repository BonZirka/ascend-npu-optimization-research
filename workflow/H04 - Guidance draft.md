# Draft guidance for answering H04

**Status: draft for review.** General workflow: [[how-to-0-to-1]]. Project answer: [[questions/H04 - Who cares and what changes if we succeed]].

## What H04 should establish

H04 asks **who cares and what changes if the project succeeds**. A reader should understand whose situation could improve and why the result matters to them.

## Working method

1. **Check existing answers first.** H01 may already describe beneficiaries and intended effects. Identify what H04 actually adds before drafting another narrative.
2. **Discuss missing consequences.** Where needed, explain what achieving the objective makes possible for the relevant people or organizations. Identify direct users and downstream beneficiaries where that distinction matters.
3. **Keep the conditions visible.** A project output may require adoption, integration or other decisions before its benefits reach people. Describe these dependencies when they affect the claim.
4. **Reuse an adequate answer.** A cross-reference is sufficient when the linked material already answers H04. Add content when it contributes a distinct consequence or useful evidence.

## Lesson from our discussion

We tried a stakeholder table, a shorter benefit summary and a list of indirect benefits. Each repeated material already discussed in H01–H03. The owner chose to make H04 a single cross-reference to H01.

The stopping criterion is coverage of the question. Separate files provide navigation; they do not require separate narratives. A reader following the reference should find the beneficiaries and intended consequences there.

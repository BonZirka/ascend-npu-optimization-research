# Draft guidance for answering H06

**Status: draft for review, based on the resource-outline stage.** General workflow: [[how-to-0-to-1]]. Project answer: [[questions/H06 - How much will it cost]]. Quantities and a monetary estimate have not yet been established for this project.

## What H06 should establish

H06 identifies **the resources required to carry out the proposed work and the basis for estimating their cost**. State the scope being estimated: an experiment, prototype or another defined result.

## Working method

1. **Start with the work.** Use the proposed scope in H03 to identify the people, equipment, services and other resources required. Choose categories appropriate to the project. Include preparation, evaluation and specialist support alongside implementation.
2. **Separate setup from operation.** Distinguish work needed to establish a usable process from resources consumed while running it. Include unsuccessful attempts where they consume resources.
3. **Establish availability.** For each resource, record what is available, what needs allocation and what remains unknown. Existing access can still consume limited capacity or staff attention.
4. **Estimate quantities before money.** Use appropriate units, such as person-days, service usage or equipment hours. Record assumptions and the source of estimates. Monetary costs can then use applicable rates; unknown quantities remain explicit.
5. **Connect uncertainty to its cause.** Link major cost uncertainties to the relevant design decisions and H05 risks. Conditional requirements stay conditional until the supporting route is established.

## Lesson from our discussion

We recorded development effort, compiler expert time, LLM usage, host compute and NPU access. These are this project's categories. Device usage depends on the compilation and checking route; access does not establish its required duration or capacity.

The result is a resource outline, with integration effort identified as a major unresolved cost. No staffing level, usage quantity or monetary budget was selected.

## Readiness check

Review whether the scope, resource categories, setup/operation distinction and major unknowns are clear. This supports further estimation. A quantified budget still requires quantities, rates and their assumptions. Keep that distinction visible when deciding whether H06 is sufficiently answered for the current proposal stage.

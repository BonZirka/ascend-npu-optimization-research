# Draft guidance for answering H07

**Status: draft for review.** General workflow: [[how-to-0-to-1]]. Project answer: [[questions/H07 - How long will it take]].

## What H07 should establish

H07 explains **when an initial usable result is expected, what follows and what assumptions support the schedule**. It connects the proposed scope to a realistic development sequence.

## Working method

1. **Define the first usable result.** State its scope and essential capabilities. An MVP or initial experiment should have a recognizable completion point. Separate optional research directions from that commitment.
2. **Order development stages.** Give each stage a purpose and an observable result, with dependencies reflected in the sequence. The mechanism described in H03 may operate repeatedly; its runtime steps need not be the project's development stages.
3. **Investigate major risks early.** Use H05 to identify uncertainties worth testing before substantial implementation. An early stage should produce evidence for deciding whether to continue or adjust the scope.
4. **Assign timeframes and state assumptions.** Record staffing, expert support, access and availability assumptions. Relate these to H06. Calendar duration depends on available capacity and dependencies as well as the amount of work.
5. **Separate the initial milestone from the overall horizon.** State whether the total duration includes the MVP. Reserve time for evaluation and give subsequent refinements a bounded scope. Choose optional work using the evidence obtained.
6. **Review the plan with the owner.** Adjust timing or scope together. Keep estimates recognizable as planning targets and make significant dependencies explicit.

## Lesson from our discussion

We first outlined ordered stages, then added a timeframe. The owner selected an MVP by the end of month 4 within a six-month overall horizon. Month 5 covers evaluation; month 6 covers one focused refinement selected from results and remaining capacity.

The staffing and access assumptions support the estimate; they do not establish actual resource allocations. These milestones belong to this project and provide an example for other proposals.

## Readiness check

A reader should be able to identify the initial deliverable, its target date, the overall horizon, stage results and major assumptions. The schedule should show where uncertainty is investigated and how later work depends on the results. Owner acceptance establishes the planning target; subsequent evidence may require revision.

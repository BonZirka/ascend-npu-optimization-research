# Draft guidance for answering H08

**Status: draft for review, based on a proposed evaluation plan.** General workflow: [[how-to-0-to-1]]. Project answer: [[questions/H08 - How will we check success]]. Concrete evaluation choices remain open.

## What H08 should establish

H08 explains **what evidence would demonstrate success, how it will be collected and what comparisons support the intended claims**. Connect the evaluation to the objective in H01, the mechanism in H03 and the milestones in H07.

## Working method

1. **Identify the primary outcome.** Discuss what matters most to the project. Distinguish that outcome from supporting measures such as activity counts, throughput or resource use. In our project, useful, reproducible findings take priority over savings in expert effort.
2. **Separate levels of evidence.** Define functional acceptance of the initial deliverable, evidence of practical usefulness and evidence of improvement over a reference. Each requires its own observations.
3. **Choose relevant evaluation cases.** Select cases, participants or conditions appropriate to the intended audience and use. Explain their relevance and keep evaluation material separate from material used to tune the approach.
4. **Agree on criteria before the main evaluation.** Define what counts as a useful result, who assesses it, applicable thresholds and resource limits. State the coverage of checks and measurements. Keep unresolved criteria visible.
5. **Record the full outcome.** Include successful, rejected, duplicate and inconclusive results, alongside relevant costs. Account for preparation and review effort when making efficiency claims. Preserve the evidence needed to inspect or reproduce results.
6. **Choose comparisons that support the claim.** For a refinement, compare against the unchanged baseline under comparable conditions, varying the feature being studied. A claim of savings in expert time needs an appropriate reference workflow. Specify repeated trials where needed to assess variation.
7. **Align evaluation with milestones.** Show what will be checked at the initial deliverable and what requires subsequent experiments. Link evaluation tasks to H07 and their resource requirements to H06.

## Lesson from our discussion

Our proposed plan separates MVP acceptance in month 4, usefulness evaluation in month 5 and refinement comparison in month 6. At least one useful, reproducible finding on a real case is a proposed initial target.

The case collection, usefulness criteria, resource limits, repeat count and reference workflow remain unestablished. Recording the plan preserves these gaps. The schedule and target are this project's example; other projects should establish their own.

## Readiness check

For each intended success claim, a reader should identify the observation, assessment method and relevant comparison, or the explicit decision still needed. The owner reviews the plan. Acceptance establishes how we propose to evaluate the work; success requires the resulting evidence.
